# Free experimental Dual Kinova distribution

Euler-Rodrigues Lab permits use and redistribution of the experimental Dual
Kinova 3 binary wheel free of charge. Builds explicitly enabling the
`free-dual-kinova3` Cargo feature require no organization license file for
Dual Kinova 3, with either Robotiq grippers or Psyonic Ability hands, and
have no expiry. An unrelated active license does not affect these sessions.

This grant covers the Dual Kinova product only. Other robot products retain
their existing license requirements. Source code remains proprietary. Included
third-party components retain their respective license terms and notices.
